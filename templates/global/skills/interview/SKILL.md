# Focused discovery

Start from the best interpretation and inspected source/documents. Resolve factual gaps with tools before asking the user. Ask focused questions only when the answer changes behavior, scope or risk.

Stop when the intended behavior and acceptance criteria are actionable. Record agreed semantic decisions in the living contract. Do not impose a fixed interview length or repeated approval for routine choices.
